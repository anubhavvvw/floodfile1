    HOW IT WORKS

    From data to early warning

    01

    Data collection

    Rainfall, water level, soil moisture and slope-risk readings are collected from four sensor zones.

    →
    02

    Fusion

    Readings are combined into one shared system state used across the whole dashboard.

    →
    03

    Risk engine

    A weighted prototype model scores the state as Normal, Warning or Critical.

    →
    04

    Early warning

    Alerts, the live map and response status update together from the same state.

    →
    05

    Response

    Affected areas, safe zones and rescue teams are tracked until the state clears.
